# A note in the margin

Mara found a formula beside the map: $d = vt$. At a steady speed of $v = 3\,\mathrm{km/h}$, the journey would take two hours.

Inline formulas use single dollar signs, like `$d = vt$`. Display formulas use double dollar signs on their own lines:

$$
\begin{aligned}
d &= vt \\
  &= 3 \times 2 \\
  &= 6\,\mathrm{km}
\end{aligned}
$$
